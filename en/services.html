001Services

Services

Four ways of working, alone or together. Each one starts with a visit and ends with a result you can see and keep.

01 / 04

Colour & materials

Choosing the colours, plasters and finishes of a place according to its light, its use and what is already there: floors, wood, stone, furniture.

What you receive

  • Reading the light, room by room, at several times of day
  • Referenced palette: brand, reference and finish for each colour
  • Painted or plastered samples, tested on site before any decision
  • Lime plasters, concrete effects and mineral finishes
  • Decorative plaster applied by Petric Sonja, or supervision of the craftsman of your choice

On quotation, after a first visit.

02 / 04

Layout & styling

Reorganising a room without structural work: circulation, furniture placement, lighting, hanging artworks and objects.

What you receive

  • Layout plan for each room
  • Mood board: colours, materials, furniture, lighting
  • Hanging plan for artworks: heights, spacing, lighting
  • What stays, what changes, what goes: a clear list
  • Installation on site, if you wish

On quotation, after a first visit.

03 / 04

Home staging

Preparing a property for sale or rental so it reads clearly in photographs and viewings: removing, rearranging, correcting colour and light.

What you receive

  • Visit and room-by-room action list, in order of priority
  • Decluttering and rearranging
  • Wall refresh before the photo shoot
  • Styling on the day of the shoot
  • Before / after photos for the owner or the agency

On quotation, after a first visit.

04 / 04

Decor & art sourcing

Finding the missing furniture, lighting, textiles, objects and artworks from craftspeople, galleries and dealers, within the budget agreed together.

What you receive

  • Selection of pieces with photos, dimensions and prices
  • Artworks and antiques: condition checked before purchase
  • Ordering, delivery and installation
  • Hanging and lighting of artworks

On quotation, after a first visit.

002Frequently asked questions

Do you draw building plans or carry out structural work?+

No. I work on colour, materials, furniture layout, light and artworks, without touching load-bearing walls or services. Structural work needs a project manager or an architect; I can work alongside them.

How does the first visit work?+

I come to see the place, listen to you, and look at the light, the volumes and what is already there. I then send you a proposal and a quotation.

How much does it cost?+

Every project is quoted individually after the first visit, depending on the size, the number of rooms and what needs doing.

Where do you work?+

In Cannes, across the French Riviera (Antibes, Nice, Grasse, Saint-Tropez and the surrounding area) and in Monaco.

Do you work with estate agents and concierge services?+

Yes. I prepare properties before the photos and viewings, and provide before / after photos for every project.

Do you also restore paintings and objects?+

Yes, it is my training: I hold a degree in the conservation-restoration of cultural property. Each work is examined first, then receives a condition report and a quotation.

Petric Sonja

Interior art direction

Petric Sonja holds a degree in the conservation-restoration of cultural property (DNSEP, Master's level, École supérieure d'art d'Avignon). There she learned to look at a work before touching it: to understand its materials, its history, what damages it and what holds it together.

She brings the same eye to interiors. Before suggesting a colour or moving a piece of furniture, she observes the light, the materials already in place and the way the space is lived in. Her proposals are restrained: change what needs changing, keep what holds.

She also paints: contemporary canvases with clean lines.

Based in Cannes. Works across the French Riviera (Antibes, Nice, Grasse, Saint-Tropez and the surrounding area) and in Monaco.

004Method

  1. 01

    Visit and listening

    I come to the place. You tell me what you expect, what you are keeping, your timing and your budget.

  2. 02

    Reading the place

    The light at different times, the volumes, the existing materials, the circulation: I look at the place before proposing anything.

  3. 03

    Proposal

    Referenced palette, mood board and layout plan, with a detailed quotation.

  4. 04

    Samples

    Colours and materials are tested on your walls and looked at several times of day before any decision.

  5. 05

    Implementation and follow-up

    I carry out or supervise the work, put the furniture and artworks in place, then photograph the result.

001Conservation

Conservation

A qualified conservator-restorer (DNSEP, École supérieure d'art d'Avignon), I work for private collectors on the works that matter to them.

002Principles

  1. 01

    Minimal intervention

    Treat only what needs treating.

  2. 02

    Reversibility

    Materials that can be removed later without harming the work.

  3. 03

    Compatibility

    Stable materials, chosen for the work and its original materials.

  4. 04

    Documentation

    Photographs before, during and after, and a written report.

  5. 05

    Respect for the work

    Its history and signs of use are part of it.

003Services

  1. 01

    Condition report

    Examination of the work and a written report with photographs: condition, damage, proposed treatment.

  2. 02

    Quotation

    Prepared after examination, detailing the proposed treatment.

  3. 03

    Studio treatment

    Cleaning, varnish reduction, consolidation, retouching, as the report requires.

  4. 04

    Preventive conservation

    Advice on hanging, lighting, humidity and handling, in your home.

On quotation, after a first visit.

004Which works?

Paintings, contemporary art, sculptures and objects, works on paper: every request is examined, and I will tell you plainly whether it is within my expertise or calls for another specialist.

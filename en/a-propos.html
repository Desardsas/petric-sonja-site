Petric Sonja

Trained at the École supérieure d'art d'Avignon, Petric Sonja divides her work between creation and craft: painting, painting restoration, decorative plaster and home staging.

Her style is contemporary and clean: simple lines, honest materials and carefully chosen colours, for works and spaces in keeping with their history.

Studio in Cannes. Works across the French Riviera: Cannes, Antibes, Nice, Grasse, Saint-Tropez and the surrounding area.

—Background

  • —DNSEP (French national higher diploma in visual arts, Master's level), Conservation-restoration of cultural property, École supérieure d'art d'Avignon

Sonja Petric

Trained at the École supérieure d'art d'Avignon, Sonja Petric divides her work between creation and craft: painting, painting restoration, decorative plaster and home staging.

Her style is contemporary and clean: simple lines, honest materials and carefully chosen colours, for works and spaces that feel like you.

Studio in Cannes. Works across the French Riviera: Cannes, Antibes, Nice, Grasse, Saint-Tropez and the surrounding area.
